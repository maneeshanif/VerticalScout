## Overview
VerticalGate is an anti-commodity venture qualification terminal. The visual language eschews standard corporate blue/green/white templates in favor of a distinctive **Cyber-Ochre & Deep Obsidian Basalt** aesthetic: Obsidian black canvas (`#090A0F`), high-impact Solar Ochre brand elements (`#F59E0B`), Cyber Amethyst analytical modules (`#8B5CF6`), and tactile interactive simulators.

## Color System
- **Obsidian Basalt Canvas:** `#090A0F` creates deep focus and maximum contrast for data visualization.
- **Solar Ochre Gold:** `#F59E0B` (`hsl(38, 95%, 55%)`) commands interactive attention as the primary brand asset.
- **Cyber Amethyst:** `#8B5CF6` encodes mathematical gates and fatal-flaw test criteria.
- **Semantic Qualification Badges:**
  - *Eligible (Good to Go):* Luminous Emerald (`#10B981` on `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.15)`).
  - *Service Domain:* Solar Amber (`#F59E0B` on `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.15)`).
  - *Parked (Commodity Trap):* Crimson Rust (`#F43F5E` on `rgba(244, 63, 94, 0.15)`).

## Interactive Surface Modules
- **Floating Capsule Nav:** Sleek glassmorphic bar anchored at the top of the viewport.
- **Live Gatekeeper Terminal:** Preset domain selector displaying real-time screen scores and beachhead slices.
- **Strategic Divergence Matrix:** 4-tab economic comparator contrasting horizontal commodity traps vs vertical monopolies.
- **Fatal-Flaw Gate Inspector:** 8-gate interactive diagnostic grid with Test 3 Expert Availability hard-stop highlighting.
- **Viability Calculator:** Interactive sliders computing viability scores dynamically.
